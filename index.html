About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Jihan Kim. I work at the interface of machine learning, molecular simulation, and materials chemistry to accelerate real-world materials discovery.

My research spans porous materials such as metal–organic frameworks (MOFs), as well as gas separation membranes, catalysis, and lithium metal batteries, much of it in close collaboration with experimental groups. I also work on inverse materials design and LLM agents for autonomous discovery.
